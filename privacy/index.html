Privacy Policy

Last updated September 20, 2026

What we collect

When you connect a social account, the app may store:

  • Account name, username, and platform user IDs
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens
  • Video files and captions you choose to publish
  • Basic post status and analytics the platform returns

Where it lives

Tokens and publish records stay on the computer that clicked Connect. This public site does not collect account tokens or video files.

How we use it

Only to connect your accounts, upload posts you approve, and show you status or analytics. We do not sell data. We do not run ads on this data.

Third parties

Connecting an account sends you to that platform’s login (TikTok, Google, Meta). Their privacy policies apply to that login.

Disconnect

Disconnect an account in the dashboard to stop using its tokens. You can also revoke access in that platform’s app settings.
